FAQ

Questions salon owners usually ask.

Is this just a salon website?

No. The website is the customer-facing layer. Salon Lead Machine connects it to booking requests, customer records, owner operations and repeat-customer activity.

Can existing customers use it?

Yes. Booking requests can be matched to an existing customer or create a new customer record, keeping the customer journey in one place.

Can customers book from different channels?

The product is designed around a unified channel model so website, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and Google-originated enquiries can feed the same booking/customer workflow as those connectors are enabled.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. The product is sold as a complete salon system. The technical setup stays behind the scenes; the owner uses the dashboard to manage bookings and customers.
